# Privacy Policy

My Kyoto does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## Information Collection
This app does not require user accounts and does not collect personal data.
All memories, photos, and notes are stored locally on the user’s device.

## Location Information
Location data is used only to display memories on the map.
This information is not stored on external servers or shared with third parties.

## Third-Party Services
My Kyoto does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.
